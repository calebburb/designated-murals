Place-Making, Not Decoration.

Designated is a mural and event design practice in Boise, Idaho, working with commercial spaces across the Treasure Valley and with touring brands on the road.

Short Bio

Hospitality First, Paint Second.

We started in event production, where the measure of good work is whether people stay. That standard carries into every wall: the mural is judged by how the room gets used afterward, not by how it photographs on day one.

Since then the practice has painted restaurants, wellness studios, and venues across Boise, and built activations and transportable work for touring brands. Every project runs on the same three stages, with a written scope before a brush is opened.
