The Experience Framework

The Design Process.

Mural, set, backdrop, or identity marker — three stages, and you know exactly what you receive at each one.

1
Consultation A 30-minute call on the atmosphere you want — subject, style, texture, color — plus a content template to introduce the installation to your audience.
2
Concepts Three digital concepts drawn from the consult, mocked onto your own wall so you can feel the space. One complimentary round of revisions.
3
Installation Protected surfaces taped off, the site secured, the approved concept painted, cleanup handled, and the finished work documented for your channels.
Event Activation

Four Stages, One Footprint.

01 · Strategy
One-hour consult on direction, audience, and conversion goals. Footprint and event plot rendering for three visual directions.
02 · Planning
Marketing assets built around the approved concept: one reel, two stills, a landing page, lead capture, and terms. Vendor and sponsor outreach begins.
03 · Activation
Vendors coordinated, operations finalized, the event executed, and set-up and teardown handled on site.
04 · Measurement
An attendance and engagement review with traceable metrics on customer engagement.
